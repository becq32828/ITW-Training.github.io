Use case. The Rain-X quarter closed with revenue up on last year and short of plan. Wiper blades and wiper fluid are both in there, and winter seasonality flatters everything. Someone senior is going to ask whether the growth is real, and the answer is sitting in a workbook nobody has opened.

Analyst Copilot in Excel Product: Rain-X Category: windshield wipers

    Watch — price, volume and mix decomposed in ninety seconds

    5 min

    Challenge — trend, or noise?

    4 min
    Challenge · stay in the same conversation and paste this
    Now make it commit: is this a trend or is it noise? Do not hedge.

    Forcing a falsifiable claim is the most advanced thing in this session. A number you can argue with beats a number you cannot.

    Stretch · ITW prompt "80/20 first cut" · Analyst or Copilot in Excel

    Sanitized data only. On the sample workbook this works as-is; on your own data, use a sanitized export.

    The move you are learning. Demand the calculation and the insights, not just the answer. An analysis you cannot check is an analysis you cannot defend.

Build a prompt worth reusing

The four moves from today, in four boxes. Fill them in and the prompt assembles itself. A prompt you wrote once and run every month is worth more than a clever one you wrote once.

MoveWhat it doesWorked on Rain-X
The decision Name what is actually at stake, not the topic. "You are preparing me for a divisional review where I decide whether to hold the Q4 Rain-X wiper price increase with our largest account or phase it."
The evidence Fence what it may use, and forbid the rest. "Use only the Rain-X wiper customer margin sheet and the pricing email thread. If something is not in those two, say 'not in the source' rather than estimating."
The adversary Demand the case against you. "Give me the strongest argument against holding the Rain-X increase, and the single fact that would change my mind."
The deliverable Say exactly what comes back. "One page. Position in two sentences, three evidence bullets each with a figure and its source, then the counter-case, then one recommendation. No adjective I cannot defend with a number."
Before and after, on the same Rain-X data
Summarise the Rain-X quarter for me.

Accurate, unobjectionable, useless. It tells you what is in the file, not what to do about it, and it collapses at the first follow-up.

I have to answer one question in Thursday's review: is Rain-X growth in windshield wipers real?

Use only the Rain-X wiper workbook and the business review memo.

Tell me what share of the growth came from price rather than volume, name every wiper line where volume fell, and quantify each.

Then give me the strongest argument that I am over-reading this, and the one fact that would change my mind.

Two-sentence position, three sourced evidence bullets, the counter-case, one recommendation.
